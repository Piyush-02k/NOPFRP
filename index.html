How FraudScore India Works

screen_search_desktop

1. Check (Prevent)

Before making a payment to an unknown UPI ID, enter it into our portal. We provide an instant, real-time Fraud Score based on crowd-sourced data and verified reports from financial institutions.

report

2. Report (Contribute)

If you've been a victim of a scam, report it through our simple, OTP-verified process. Your report immediately helps to flag the fraudulent ID, protecting others from falling for the same scam.

leaderboard

3. Track (Resolve)

We provide a transparent timeline for your complaint, showing its journey from initial report to bank notification and cyber crime escalation. No more being left in the dark.

Our Commitment to Privacy

We are built on trust. We NEVER store sensitive personal data like account balances or full transaction histories. Our system only analyzes UPI ID metadata to fight fraud, ensuring your identity remains secure and anonymous.
